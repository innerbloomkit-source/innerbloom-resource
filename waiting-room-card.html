What To Listen For

If the appointment goes well

A doctor who explores cycle timing, acknowledges the limits of standard blood testing, and treats your symptoms as a connected pattern rather than separate complaints is one worth returning to. Note what they say. Ask them to document their reasoning.

If it does not go well

A doctor who dismisses these questions, or offers an antidepressant without exploring the hormonal component first, is not giving you the standard of care you deserve.

You are not being difficult. You are asking legitimate clinical questions — and you have every right to a second opinion from a doctor who understands perimenopausal hormone fluctuation.
